NP Membership Benefits

Cost: $0 — Designed for non-profit organizations.

  • Listed in chamber directory
  • Invitation to quarterly non-profit roundtable
  • Access to community service events
  • Monthly newsletter feature

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $100/year — Perfect for small businesses.

  • All NP benefits
  • Business training workshops (2 per year)
  • 10% discount on chamber events
  • Social media spotlight (1 per quarter)
  • Access to member-only networking

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $200/year — Most popular for growing businesses.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Business training workshops (unlimited)
  • 25% discount on chamber events
  • Social media spotlight (monthly)
  • Home page spotlight rotation
  • Advertising in chamber newsletter

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $400/year — Maximum visibility and influence.

  • All Silver benefits
  • 50% discount on all chamber events
  • Premium home page spotlight (top 3)
  • Keynote opportunity at annual gala
  • Dedicated business advocacy
  • Free booth at Holiday Market & Makers Fair
  • Priority referral program
