WDD 231 • Discover • Timbuktu, Mali

Discover Timbuktu

Ancient crossroads of trade, knowledge, and culture at the edge of the Sahara. Eight factual, representative sites — demographics, history, and commerce — curated for prospective members and visitors. Population ~54,000 • UNESCO World Heritage • Niger River corridor.

Why Timbuktu Matters for Business

Median age 16.5 • 3 historic mosques • 700,000 manuscripts preserved • River trade + caravan heritage • Festival tourism + artisan markets. Your Chamber membership supports restoration, education, and sustainable trade routes.
